Questions

Does it connect to my bank?

Not out of the box. It reads a snapshot file you produce, so your data never touches a server you don't control.

What's it built with?

TypeScript, React, TanStack Start, Tailwind and Recharts. It deploys to Vercel or anywhere Node runs.

Is the coaching financial advice?

No. Liněn and its coaching bot are educational tools, not a licensed financial adviser.
