Good to know

Does it connect to my bank?

Not out of the box. It reads a file you export from your bank, so your data never sits on someone else's computer.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The kit comes with a copy-and-paste prompt so an AI assistant can set it up and put it online for you, free.

Is the coaching financial advice?

No. Liněn and Marcus are educational tools, not a licensed financial adviser.
